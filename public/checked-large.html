Large Checked Capacity

A 28-32" large checked bag typically holds:

  • 10-14 days of clothing
  • Multiple pairs of shoes
  • Bulky items (coats, boots)
  • Toiletries and personal items
  • Room for souvenirs

Weight Management Tips

Large bags make it easy to exceed weight limits. Here's how to stay under:

  • Weigh before you pack - Know your bag's empty weight first
  • Use a luggage scale - Weigh packed bags at home and before returning
  • Wear heavy items - Boots and heavy coats don't count toward luggage weight
  • Distribute weight - Split between two medium bags if you're over
  • Ship items home - For heavy souvenirs, shipping may be cheaper than overweight fees

Oversized Bag Fees

Most airlines consider bags over 62 linear inches (length + width + height) as oversized. Check your bag's dimensions and your airline's policy to avoid surprise fees of $100-200 per bag.
