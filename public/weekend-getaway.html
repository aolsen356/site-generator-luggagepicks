Packing Tips for Weekend Trips

Making the most of your compact luggage starts with smart packing. Here are our top tips:

  • Roll your clothes - Rolling takes up less space than folding and reduces wrinkles
  • Wear your bulkiest items - Save space by wearing your heaviest shoes and jacket on the plane
  • Use packing cubes - Keep items organized and compressed for maximum space efficiency
  • Stick to a color scheme - Choose versatile pieces that mix and match easily
  • Minimize toiletries - Decant into travel-sized containers or buy at your destination

Why Carry-On Only?

Traveling with just a carry-on has several advantages:

  • No checked bag fees (save $30-60 per round trip)
  • No waiting at baggage claim
  • Zero risk of lost luggage
  • Easier mobility through airports and to your destination
  • Forces you to pack light and avoid overpacking
