Choosing the Right Set Size

2-Piece Sets

Typically include a carry-on and one checked bag. Best for solo travelers or couples who don't need maximum capacity. Most versatile combination for short and medium trips.

3-Piece Sets (Most Popular)

Usually include a carry-on (20-22"), medium checked (24-26"), and large checked (28-30"). Covers all travel scenarios from weekend trips to extended vacations. Best value for most travelers.

5-Piece Sets

Include all sizes plus accessories like toiletry bags or packing cubes. Best for families or those who want a complete travel system. Often includes pieces you may never use.

Set Buying Tips

  • Consider storage - Nesting sets (where smaller bags fit inside larger ones) save closet space
  • Check individual dimensions - Ensure each piece meets your airline's size requirements
  • Look for consistent quality - Wheels, handles, and zippers should be the same across all pieces
  • Choose distinctive colors - Matching sets in common colors (black, silver) can be hard to identify at baggage claim
  • Read reviews for each size - Sometimes one piece in a set has issues while others are fine

Nested Storage

Many sets nest for storage: the carry-on fits inside the medium, which fits inside the large. This saves significant closet space when not traveling. Before buying, check if the set nests and approximately how much space it saves.
