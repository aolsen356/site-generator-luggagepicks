Best Checked Luggage

Find the right checked bag for your trip. Choose between medium bags for week-long trips or large bags for extended travel.

Checked Bag Basics

Most airlines allow checked bags up to 62 linear inches (length + width + height) and 50 lbs. Choosing the right size means maximizing packing capacity while staying under weight limits.

Choose Your Size

💳

Medium Checked (24-26")

The versatile choice for week-long trips. Light enough to maneuver easily, spacious enough for 5-7 days of clothing.

Best for: 5-7 day trips, solo travelers, easy handling
💳

Large Checked (28-32")

Maximum capacity for extended trips. Room for 10+ days of clothing plus souvenirs on the way home.

Best for: 10+ day trips, families, bringing home souvenirs

Medium vs. Large: Quick Comparison

Factor Medium (24-26") Large (28-32")
Typical Weight 8-10 lbs empty 10-13 lbs empty
Packing Capacity 40+ lbs of items 37-40 lbs of items
Trip Length 5-7 days 10-14 days
Maneuverability Easy More challenging when full
Overweight Risk Lower Higher - watch your weight

Checked Bag Tips

  • Weigh before you go: Use a luggage scale at home to avoid airport overweight fees
  • Know the empty weight: Subtract bag weight from 50 lbs to know your true capacity
  • Pack valuables in carry-on: Never check electronics, jewelry, or irreplaceable items
  • Use ID tags inside and out: External tags can fall off; internal ID ensures recovery
  • Consider bright colors: Easier to spot at baggage claim and less likely to be grabbed by mistake
