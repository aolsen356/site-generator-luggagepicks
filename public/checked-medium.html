Medium Checked Capacity

A 24-26" medium checked bag typically holds:

  • 5-7 days of clothing
  • 2-3 pairs of shoes
  • Toiletries and personal items
  • Electronics and accessories
  • Light outerwear

Weight Considerations

With a bag weighing 8-10 lbs empty and a 50 lb airline limit, you have 40+ lbs of packing capacity. This is more than enough for a week of travel, with room to spare for souvenirs on the return trip.

Avoiding Overweight Fees

  • Weigh your bag at home before leaving
  • Wear heavy items (boots, jacket) on the plane
  • Move heavy items to carry-on if needed at check-in
  • Consider a portable luggage scale for the return trip
