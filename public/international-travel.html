International Travel Considerations

Weight Limits

International flights often have stricter weight limits than domestic. Many international carriers limit checked bags to 23kg (50 lbs), with some budget airlines allowing only 15-20kg. Always check your airline's specific requirements.

Carry-On Size Variations

International carry-on limits vary widely. While US airlines generally allow 22" x 14" x 9", many international carriers (especially budget airlines) enforce smaller limits. See our international airline guide for specifics.

Security and Locks

TSA-approved locks are essential for flights to, from, or through the United States. TSA agents can open these locks without breaking them. For international-only travel, any lock works.

Warranties That Travel

Choose brands with global warranty networks. Briggs & Riley, Samsonite, and TUMI have international repair centers that can fix damage while you're abroad.

Essential International Travel Tips

  • Use a luggage scale - Weigh bags at home to avoid surprise overweight fees
  • Keep valuables in carry-on - Never check electronics, jewelry, or important documents
  • Photograph your luggage - Document contents before departure for insurance claims
  • Add inside and outside ID - Include contact info in destination country
  • Pack a day's essentials in carry-on - Be prepared if checked luggage is delayed
